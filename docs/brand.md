# FrameForge Brand Assets

## Primary Logo

Use the primary FrameForge logo for repository documentation, marketing pages, product headers, and launch materials.

![FrameForge logo](../assets/brand/logo.svg)

## Usage Notes

- Prefer the SVG asset at `assets/brand/logo.svg` for crisp rendering at any size.
- Preserve the dark background, neon film-strip `F`, play button, wordmark, and tagline when using the primary lockup.
- Do not stretch, recolor, or crop the logo in ways that remove the film-strip or play-button motif.
- For small UI contexts, derive a separate icon-only asset from the film-strip `F` rather than scaling the full wordmark down until the tagline becomes unreadable.
